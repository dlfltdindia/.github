# DLF Westpark Mumbai – A Look at Luxury Homes in Andheri West

When searching for a home in Mumbai, buyers usually consider more than just the size of an apartment. The neighbourhood, nearby facilities, travel convenience and overall living experience are equally important.

For those researching premium housing options in the western suburbs, [DLF Westpark](https://dlfltd.in/dlf-westpark-mumbai-andheri-west) is a project worth exploring. Located along New Link Road in Andheri West, just opposite Infiniti Mall, it offers 3 and 4 BHK apartments with contemporary layouts and lifestyle facilities.

## Project Introduction

DLF Westpark is a large residential development covering approximately 18 acres. The project is being developed by DLF Home Developers Ltd in association with Trident Realty.

The development is planned with high-rise towers featuring G+37 floors. Its location in Andheri West provides residents with access to shopping destinations, public transport and various daily necessities.

Different families have different space requirements, which is why apartment configurations are an important part of property selection.

DLF Westpark offers the following residential options:
