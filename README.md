## DLF The Aureva Sector 63 Gurugram: A Thoughtful Approach to Luxury Senior Living
Choosing a home is not only about its size, location or appearance. For people entering their retirement years, comfort, independence and access to healthcare become equally important. Keeping these needs in mind, [DLF The Aureva](https://www.dlfltd.in/dlf-the-aureva-sector-63-gurgaon) in Sector 63, Gurugram, presents a residential concept focused on senior living. Developed by DLF, the project brings together spacious homes, senior-friendly design and healthcare facilities in one residential address.

# Understanding DLF The Aureva
DLF The Aureva is a luxury senior living project located on Golf Course Extension Road in Sector 63, Gurugram. The development is planned for residents who want to enjoy a comfortable lifestyle while maintaining their independence during their later years.

The project occupies approximately 4.17 acres and features a single residential tower with 172 residences. With only four homes planned on each floor, the layout focuses on providing a more private residential environment.

Unlike conventional residential developments, DLF The Aureva places particular attention on the everyday requirements of senior residents. Its planning includes accessible spaces, dedicated medical facilities and residential features intended to make daily movement more convenient.

# Spacious Residences Designed for Comfort
One of the key aspects of DLF The Aureva Sector 63 Gurugram is its spacious residential configuration. The project offers 4 BHK homes with a staff dormitory, covering approximately 4,121 square feet of saleable area.

The residences are planned with two master suites, each featuring wheelchair-accessible washrooms. Wider foyers are also included to support easier movement, including access for wheelchairs and stretchers.

A staff dormitory provides space for up to three staff members within each residence. This arrangement may be useful for households that require additional domestic or personal assistance.

The homes also feature private decks, with depths of up to nine feet, offering residents an additional space to relax. A floor-to-floor height of approximately 3.4 metres adds to the sense of openness within the residences.

These design elements reflect an approach that considers both residential comfort and the practical needs of senior homeowners.

# Healthcare Facilities Within the Residential Environment
Healthcare access is an important consideration when selecting a senior living residence. DLF The Aureva includes dedicated medical infrastructure as part of its overall planning.

The project features an approximately 7,000-square-foot medical centre located on a separate parcel. A medical room of around 1,076 square feet is also planned within the residential tower.

According to the project details, healthcare services are intended to include preventive care, specialist consultations and emergency ambulance services. The medical facilities are planned to be managed by an experienced healthcare operator.

Having medical infrastructure within the residential environment can offer greater convenience for residents and their families. It also reflects the project's focus on creating a living arrangement that considers changing lifestyle requirements over time.

DLF The Aureva includes an approximately 30,000-square-foot clubhouse with lifestyle and recreational facilities. The listed amenities include:

The development also features an eco-bridge connecting the residences with the clubhouse and medical centre. This planned connection brings key facilities together within the project's layout.

Approximately one acre of landscaped greens is included in the project, providing outdoor spaces within the residential surroundings.

# Location Advantages of Sector 63, Gurugram
Sector 63 is situated along Golf Course Extension Road, a residential corridor in Gurugram that has attracted several premium housing developments.

The location provides access to the Southern Peripheral Road, which connects the area with Sohna Road and NH-48. This road network is relevant for residents travelling to different parts of Gurugram and the wider Delhi NCR region.

The surrounding area also includes educational, healthcare and retail destinations. According to the project page, Worldmark is approximately 1.3 kilometres away, Heritage School is around 2.4 kilometres away, and Marengo Asia Hospital is approximately 3.9 kilometres from the project.

These nearby destinations can be useful for residents and visiting family members, depending on their individual travel requirements.
